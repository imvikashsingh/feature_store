**“Not every customer is equally valuable—and not every churn deserves the same response. The real challenge isn’t predicting who will leave; 
it’s knowing which customer you simply cannot afford to lose.”**

Our current churn model evaluates every savings account customer in the same way and triggers similar retention actions across the board. 
While that approach performs well statistically, it overlooks an important business reality: a relatively small group of high-value customers—those with balances above **€100,000**—holds nearly **60% of the bank’s total deposits**.

In this presentation, I’ll show how we can transform churn prediction from a generic machine learning exercise into a strategic business capability.
By combining customer transaction behavior with external postal-code tax insights and introducing targeted modeling for high-value customers, 
we can identify the customers who matter most and intervene with personalized retention strategies before they leave.

More importantly, success will be measured not just by model accuracy, but by the value we preserve: 
stronger retention of high-value customers, smarter allocation of incentives, and a measurable impact on the bank’s bottom line.

**“In the end, the best churn model isn’t the one that predicts the most departures—it’s the one that protects the greatest value.”**
